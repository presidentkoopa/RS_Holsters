# RS_Holsters

Reach down. Grab your hip. There's a gun there.

Up to eight body-anchored holsters — hip, head-side, pectoral, and a second hip pair — that live in physical space around you, not in a menu. Grip a weapon near one and it locks in place, glowing and ready. Reach back for it later with either hand and it's exactly where you left it. Pick how many you want live — 2, 4, 6, or all 8 — right from the options menu.

This isn't a keybind pretending to be a holster. It's a real VR interaction system built down into the engine itself: a native grip arbiter resolves whether your hand is stabilizing a two-handed weapon, holding a modifier, or reaching for gear — all from the *same* physical grip button, with zero stolen input. Nothing else in the DoomXR ecosystem does this.

### What it does

- **Grip to store, grip to draw** — either hand, any holster, no menus
- **2, 4, 6, or 8 holsters** — dial in how many you want live, from hip-only up to the full set
- **Reticle markers** at every holster — a bracket or the original wireframe sphere, your call — always visible, always accurate to the real grab radius, and recolorable to whatever you like
- **Live position editing** — grab a marker in-headset and drag it onto your actual body instead of guessing at proportions from a chart
- **Seated & standing profiles** — save both, flip between them with one press, and your eye height re-calibrates for whichever one you're in
- **Instant store/draw** (optional) — skips the usual half-second weapon-lower animation just for holster swaps, so both hands can work at once without fighting each other
- **A sound with a home** — every clunk and confirm chime plays from the holster itself, not glued to your head
- **Fully tunable** — scale, orientation, sound, timing, even the marker's shape and color, all from a real options menu

### Requires

- RS_Main (for the weapon models the holsters borrow)
- The UZDXREMA engine build (for the native holster fields this mod is built on)

Still actively getting tuned — but the core is solid: reach, grip, it's there.
